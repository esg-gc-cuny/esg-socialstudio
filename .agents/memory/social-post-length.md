---
name: Social post length handling
description: Preserve readability while fitting short, platform-specific social posts.
---

Never fit a social teaser by cutting in the middle of a sentence. Prefer complete sentences; if an overlong hook still matters, shorten at a grammatical clause boundary. Reserve space for the source URL, hashtags, and call to action before choosing the hook length.

Start posts directly with a topic-specific teaser paragraph, not an announcement such as “New on the website.” When a source URL is available, put platform-specific hashtags before the final line: “Read the full article on our website: [URL].” Keep the teaser itself in third person; this fixed CTA may use “our.”

**Why:** Character targets, especially posts with a long article URL, can otherwise leave a dangling phrase that reads like an error.

**How to apply:** When generating or postprocessing social copy, budget the final CTA and hashtags first, then keep complete hook sentences within the remaining space. Keep Bluesky hooks especially concise and inspect the final copied post. Keep the fixed CTA and hashtags in the same order in the preview and copied text.

For ESG Social Studio, match the article's voice while writing as the publisher in third person. Analyze the article internally, generate platform-specific candidates, and show one selected teaser per platform. Use limited context and one key detail rather than summarizing the full article; keep platform-specific length and hashtag rules.

**Why:** The user approved a simple one-draft-per-platform workflow and emphasized human, publisher-voice teasers that hook readers without giving away the full article.

**How to apply:** Keep article analysis and alternate drafts out of the current UI unless requested. Preserve the final article-link line and only use hashtags where the platform calls for them.
